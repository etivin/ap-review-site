Hex Web — Hexagonal Thinking

Every Unit 2 idea is a hexagon. Start from the center and branch outward — each hexagon can touch up to six others, so your web can grow in any direction. Wherever two hexagons share an edge, a connection point appears; click it and say how the two ideas are linked. You earn points for every connection you explain — more for writing your reasoning, using different connection types, and building hub ideas that branch three or more ways. There's no single right map: the thinking is in the connections you can defend.
