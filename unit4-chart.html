Unit 4 · Transoceanic Interconnections · c. 1450 – c. 1750

Chart the Voyage

Eight great voyages, one blank map. For each voyage you get the starting port and the destination: draw the route the ships actually sailed. The closer your line is to the real route, the more points you earn. Then read the ship’s log to learn why they went that way.

How to play

1. DrawPress on the starting port and drag along the route to the destination. Lift and keep going if you need to; Undo removes the last stroke.
2. SubmitYour line is compared with the real route along its whole length, up to 100 points per voyage. A hint shows a stop along the way but costs 15 points.
3. Bonus roundAfter the voyages, draw the Treaty of Tordesillas line and sort lands between Spain and Portugal for up to 160 more points.
